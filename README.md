# Our Little Campground

A tiny, cozy co-op game for two players. Arrive at a small, neglected woodland campground, tidy it up together,
restore the guest pitches, decorate, welcome visitors, and leave time to fish and sit by the fire.

- Felt-and-fabric miniature look
- Two players: on one PC with a dynamic split screen, or on two PCs online
- Keyboard and mouse or a PS5 DualSense controller
- Made with Godot 4.7.2 (GDScript)

Early development: the plan is in [`docs/PLAN.md`](docs/PLAN.md) and progress in [`docs/STATUS.md`](docs/STATUS.md).

## Run it
Open the folder in [Godot 4.7.2](https://godotengine.org/download) and press F5.

## Tests
```
godot --headless --import
godot --headless --script res://tests/run_tests.gd
```
